FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this a life jacket?

No. It's an impact vest and it is not a US Coast Guard approved life jacket. It adds some flotation and protection for wakesurfing, but it does not meet Coast Guard requirements. Most states require an approved life jacket for waterskiing, wakeboarding, tubing and riding a personal watercraft, and every boat needs one approved life jacket per person aboard. Check your state's rules before you ride.

Do I pay anything now?

No. A reservation is free and needs no card. If your print unlocks, we email you a payment link at the pre-order price. You decide then.

What if my print doesn't hit ?

Then it isn't made and your reservation is cancelled. You're never charged. We'll tell you which prints made it and you can switch to one of those.

When does it ship, and where?

US addresses only for now. Exact shipping cost and delivery timing are in the payment email, before you pay. Each vest is made to order, so expect a few weeks, not a few days.

How does sizing work?

Adult S to XXL, sized by chest. . The final chart comes with the production samples, and you can change your size up until you pay.

Can I design my own print?

Not yet. Custom one-off vests are on the list. Join the drop list below and you'll hear first.

Will the print fade?

The print is dyed into the fabric rather than sitting on top, so it won't crack or peel. Rinse with fresh water and dry it out of direct sun to keep the colours bright longer.
